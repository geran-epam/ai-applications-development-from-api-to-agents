##USER QUESTION:
What is the ECO function on this microwave and how do you activate it?

🤖 Answer:
The ECO function is the microwave’s power-saving mode.

To activate it:
1. Press the DISINFECT/DEODORIZE/ECO button until the display shows the power-saving mode code “ECO.”
2. Press START/QUICK START to confirm.

To cancel power-saving mode, open or close the oven door, press any button, or turn the MENU/TIME dial once.
